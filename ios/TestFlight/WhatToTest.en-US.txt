New and changed

- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media controls are closer to the other OpenCine apps. Cameras > Add setup opens the same chooser, with USB-C and camera Wi-Fi beside Wi-Fi and Hotspot.
- DISP 3 returns with a REC chip, large camera timecode and larger setting tiles, in place of the top bar. Tap a tile to change it; hold and drag to reorder.
- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting, Select all, transfer progress and playback no longer stall. With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip keeps downloading after you close it and reads CACHED.
- Tapping the storage readout steps through free space, free percentage and estimated time, with the same text in portrait and landscape. In Photo, SHOTS shows a short count such as 6.7k, and a tap shows free space and percentage.
- Instant review reads the preview stored inside RAW photos, appears sooner for RAW, JPEG and RAW+JPEG in every exposure mode, and RAW+JPEG reviews turn sharp.
- The FPS chip reads LINK until the first picture, then the frame rate. In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec, and portrait readouts use a three-column grid. Connection diagnostics distinguish incoming video, decoding and display interruptions.

Fixes

- The live picture runs smoothly after connecting: a running timecode no longer bogs down the screen. Live View keeps camera readouts and timecode frames through a warm restart or Quality Bias change.
- Background recovery keeps the stills or video mode you were using, and the camera is no longer kept busy while the app is in the background.
- A dozing camera gets a waiting step of up to about 20 s; half-press the shutter during it to connect. A camera that stays silent says it may be asleep instead of showing a connection error.
- Take Over asks first, and the phone that loses the camera stops reconnecting for good. A refused camera Wi-Fi join offers Settings, an explicit retry asks again, and an abandoned connection no longer carries on behind the setup screen.
- Exposure readouts are restored: Auto ISO shows its limit, values the camera's program owns are dimmed and locked, hold on SHUTTER toggles its lock, and Reading camera settings shows while live view starts.
- Photo mode turns the LUT and the cinema tools off; video restores them. Shots left follow the stills card, app-triggered photos use that card, and in CL/CH a tap takes one photo while a hold takes a burst.
- Media sharing treats photos as photos and finishes a clip's copy first. Playback stays paused after Share or app return. The GYRO pill no longer covers the screen lock, and tapping to focus during a focus pull no longer leaves it busy.
- VoiceOver skips collapsed assist controls and live controls under a covering screen, and shutter labels describe the action. Setup asks for Local Network at its own step. The open landscape View Assist palette hides the values it covers.

What to test

- In video mode, rotate and inspect DISP 3, the FPS chip (LINK, then the frame rate), timecode frames and camera readouts. Tap the storage readout through its steps, then switch to Photo and check SHOTS.
- Try instant review with RAW, JPEG and RAW+JPEG in A, S, M and P. In CL/CH, tap once, then hold. Check the stills card and Photo/video LUT behavior.
- Open Media on a full card: scroll, select, use Select all and cache a few clips. With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip briefly, go back and wait about 30 s: it reads CACHED.
- Let the camera doze, then connect: it wakes and connects, or shows the asleep message if it stays silent. Retry a cancelled Wi-Fi join; with two phones, Take Over asks first and the first phone stops.
- Change Quality Bias, background the app and return; Live View stays usable. If the live picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time and what you were doing.
