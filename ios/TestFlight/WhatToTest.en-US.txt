New and changed

- In portrait, the large format readout now lines up exactly with the clock at the top of the screen.

Fixes

- Tapping to focus while the focus dial is driving no longer leaves focus stuck with a camera busy message.
- After the landscape assist palette collapses, its hidden buttons are no longer reachable with VoiceOver.
- Using a volume button as the remote shutter is protected against a rare unexpected quit.

What to test

- Rotate to portrait and check that the format readout and the clock share the same top edge, and that every status readout is visible.
- In landscape, open and collapse the assist palette. With VoiceOver on, check that only the visible buttons are announced.
- Turn the on-screen focus dial, then tap a new focus point mid-turn, several times. Focus should keep responding without a busy message.
- Use a volume button as the remote shutter to start and stop a recording; report any unexpected quit.
