New and changed

- Camera controls and Media now use the shared OpenCine screens, so they look and behave as in the other OpenCine camera apps.
- Exposure, shutter angle, storage and link readouts follow the values supplied by the camera.
- Recording duration follows confirmed recording state.
- Live View and Multicam share consistent clock and camera-control presentation.
- Record the phone's gyro alongside Nikon camera takes; review, share, delete or export motion logs for Gyroflow.
- Calibrate a firmly mounted phone, including tilted holders, or choose a mount preset. Saved alignment applies to future takes.

Fixes

- Returning from Media restores the selected camera's live picture.
- Storage readouts retain reported precision and mark missing measurements as unavailable.
- Interrupted gyro takes preserve available motion data for recovery after a disconnect, backgrounding or app restart.
- After a disconnect, recovery keeps the camera in the stills or video mode you were using.
- In portrait, the status readouts use a three-column grid that grows with the number of rows.

What to test

- Connect and compare exposure, storage, link and recording time with the camera. Check landscape first, then rotate and compare Live View and Multicam clocks.
- Fix the phone firmly to the camera. In Phone mount, capture three calibration poses, holding still before each capture.
- Enable gyro logging and wait for GYRO ARMED. Record a short clip, check the saved log, open Media, then return to Live View and reconnect.
- Background the app or reconnect during a take. Check the interrupted log when you return.
- With camera and lens stabilization off, export a short fixed-focal-length take and a ten-minute take. Choose a lens profile and sync in Gyroflow; check the beginning, middle and end.
- Disconnect and reconnect the camera in stills mode, then in video mode, and check the mode is kept. Rotate to portrait and check the status readouts.
