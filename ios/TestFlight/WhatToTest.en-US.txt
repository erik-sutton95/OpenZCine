New and changed

- Display settings are rebuilt around the three DISP modes, with separate video and photo layouts (one switch at the top of the tab). DISP 1 and DISP 2 have an "Edit view" that opens the monitor itself: tap the eye on any element to show or hide it, down to individual readouts and buttons. Hidden items stay on screen at 30% while you edit.
- DISP 2 offers the same elements with different defaults: picture, side rail, focus box and batteries, everything else off. The recording tally, the record control while rolling and camera warnings always stay.
- The focus dial works in video as well as photo - off by default, in the FOCUS popup. Live view also gains a magnifier: switch it on in the view-assist tools, pick 2x, 3x or 4x, and a key punches in on the focus point and back out.
- On iPad, the picture can come from the camera's HDMI output through a USB capture device - full quality, none of the live-view compression - while exposure, focus, record and media keep working over the camera link. Pick Cable Link when connecting, or switch source in Settings > Link. H.265 8-bit and 10-bit are separate choices: the codec popup shows one H.265 row with 8-bit and 10-bit either side. Stream Preset and Quality Bias both read Fast, Balanced, Quality - Quality Bias had two positions before. New installs start on Quality.
- Playback gains a clean-view button that hides every control, with one small button in the corner to bring them back. High-frame-rate clips can be watched at their edit speed - pick a rate and it shows what you get, e.g. 60 to 24 fps at 40%. De-squeeze now works in photo mode too. The star rating no longer takes a permanent band of picture: tap the star handle at the top to show the stars, tap again to hide them.
- Media filters match the tab you are on: Photos offers JPEG, HEIF and NEF, an L/M/S size row, and Today / 7 days / 30 days.

Fixes

- Changes you make on the camera reach the app quickly again. Turning the aperture ring, ISO or shutter used to take up to twenty seconds to show - or in video, never.
- Photos shot as HEIF now appear in the media browser at all. The app did not recognise the file type Nikon writes, so they were silently missing from the list.
- Connecting a second camera no longer changes its settings. Moving between bodies could flip shutter angle back to shutter speed; connecting is now read-only.
- Pickers show what your camera supports, in the order it uses. Bodies without U1-U3 are no longer offered them, and drive modes read Single, CL, CH, CH+.
- Unplugging the cable or power-cycling the camera used to wedge the app until you force-quit it. Both now recover on their own, or offer Retry connection on the last frame.
- The first connection attempt no longer needs cancelling and retrying.
- Command view keeps the timecode running. It used to freeze at whatever the clock read when you switched - convincing, and wrong while rolling.
- The focus dial no longer stalls and takes tap-to-focus down with it, and the media filter popup fits on smaller phones.

What to test

- Open Settings, Display, DISP 2, then Edit view. Hide a few things - a rail key, the focus box, a readout - tap Done and confirm the live monitor matches. Check DISP 1 kept its own layout.
- Switch the magnifier on, set it to 3x, and punch in while pulling focus by hand. Check the picture goes back exactly where it was when you punch out. On iPad, plug in an HDMI capture device and try Cable Link > HDMI, then Settings > Link > Picture Source to switch source mid-session.
- On the Photos tab, open Filter: you should see JPEG/HEIF/NEF and L/M/S, not MOV and 6K.
- Turn the aperture ring while watching IRIS, in video and in photo. It should track you as you turn, close to real time.
- Connect one body, disconnect, then connect a differently configured one: nothing on the second camera should change. Then pull the cable mid-shot - it should hold the last frame and offer Retry.
