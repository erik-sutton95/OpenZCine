New and changed

- A new shared OpenCine interface for the monitor, camera list, settings and media player, with compact View Assist captions and a new app icon.
- Live View and Multicam show timecode frames when the camera supplies them; cameras without frame timecode keep the seconds display.
- Format keeps image area, resolution and compatible frame rates grouped, with codec as a separate control.

Fixes

- Media clip taps stay usable after a camera source changes or reconnects.
- On iPhone and iPad, playback waits for missing clip data and resumes when it arrives. Pause keeps it stopped; an interrupted transfer reports an error.
- Large camera clips use their full file size and keep partial downloads for retry. A camera without large-file support reports an error instead of repeating clip data.

What to test

- Reconnect, open Media and try short, long and partially downloaded clips in portrait and both landscape directions.
- On a clip that is still downloading, watch playback wait for more data, then press Pause. It should restart only when you press Play.
- Check timecode against the camera display, including frame zero. Try a clip over 4 GB if available and report any transfer error.
