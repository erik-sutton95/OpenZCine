New and changed

- Live stream the monitor picture to YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Instagram or your own server: Settings > Sharing > Live stream, then Go live under the timecode. Not yet tested on a real stream.
- Multiview: watch, set and record up to four Z bodies on one screen. Tap the grid icon in Your cameras. Not yet tested on camera hardware.
- Camera presets in Settings > Controls save and recall exposure and white balance. GUIDES add Action safe and Title safe, and the monitor lock also holds the screen rotation.

Fixes

- In N-Log the highlight zebra can go above 234, for example 245 to match the camera.
- VoiceOver no longer reads the monitor's header readouts twice.

What to test

- Save a stream key, then Go live over USB-C, a shared Wi-Fi network or the phone's hotspot. Check the stream plays and Stop ends it. On the camera's own Wi-Fi, Go live should say why it is off.
- Join two or more bodies to the phone's network, open Multiview, add them and record. Use Share Diagnostics if a tile drops.
- Save a camera preset, change those values on the camera, then recall it. Lock the monitor upside down and level the phone: the picture should stay put.
