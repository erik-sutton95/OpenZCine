New and changed

- Record the phone's gyro alongside Nikon camera takes and export motion logs for stabilization in Gyroflow.
- Calibrate a firmly mounted phone, including tilted holders, or choose a mount preset. Saved alignment applies to future takes.
- Review, share and delete saved takes in Gyro logs.

Fixes

- Interrupted gyro takes preserve available motion data for recovery after a disconnect, backgrounding or app restart.

What to test

- Fix the phone firmly to the camera. In Phone mount, capture the three calibration poses, holding still before each capture.
- Enable gyro logging, wait for GYRO ARMED, then start and stop a camera recording. Check that a saved gyro log appears.
- Background the app or reconnect during a take. Check the interrupted log when you return.
- With camera and lens stabilization off, export a short fixed-focal-length take and a ten-minute take. Choose the lens profile and sync in Gyroflow; check the beginning, middle and end.
