New and changed

- A more consistent OpenCine interface brings camera setup, settings, View Assist and media controls closer together across the apps.
- DISP 3 returns with a REC chip, large camera timecode and larger setting tiles. Tap to change a setting; hold and drag to reorder.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- The FPS chip uses the same words as the other apps: LINK until the first picture arrives, then the frame rate. It no longer shows READY or BUSY.
- Instant review reads the preview stored inside RAW photos. Connection diagnostics distinguish incoming video, decoding and display interruptions.
- Cameras > Add setup opens the same chooser as the other OpenCine apps, with USB-C and camera Wi-Fi beside Wi-Fi and Hotspot.

Fixes

- Instant review appears sooner for RAW, JPEG and RAW+JPEG across exposure modes, and RAW+JPEG reviews turn sharp. In CL/CH, tap for one photo or hold for a burst.
- Photo mode turns the LUT off; video restores it. Shots left follow the stills card, and app-triggered photos use that card.
- The GYRO pill no longer covers the screen lock. Gyro logging waits for video mode, and tapping to focus during a focus pull no longer leaves it busy.
- Live View keeps camera readouts and timecode frames through a warm restart or Quality Bias change. Background recovery preserves the camera mode.
- A dozing camera is woken and dialled once more instead of failing as unreachable. Saved-camera connections can wake the camera and ask before taking over.
- An explicit retry asks again after a refused camera Wi-Fi join. An abandoned connection no longer carries on behind the setup screen.
- Media opens large cards without freezing. Sharing treats photos as photos, finishes a clip's copy first, and playback stays paused after Share or app return.
- VoiceOver skips collapsed assist controls and live controls under a covering screen. Shutter labels describe the action; setup asks for Local Network at its own step.

What to test

- In video mode, rotate and inspect DISP 3, the FPS chip (LINK, then the frame rate), timecode frames and camera readouts. Switch to Photo and check shots and storage.
- Try instant review with RAW, JPEG and RAW+JPEG in A, S, M and P. In CL/CH, tap once, then hold. Check the stills card and Photo/video LUT behavior.
- Open Media on a full card and scroll. Share a clip and check playback stays paused. Change Quality Bias, background and return; Live View stays usable.
- Let the camera doze, then connect: it wakes and connects, or shows the asleep or off-network message if it stays silent. Retry a cancelled Wi-Fi join; Take Over asks first.
- If the live picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time, what you were doing and whether camera controls still responded.
