New and changed

- Focus offers Native AF for saved subjects and Timed Rack for repeatable A/B moves. Native AF uses the camera's g6 AF speed. Timed Rack shows requested and expected times; Prepare lens measures timing between sharp marks with recording stopped.
- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media share controls with the other OpenCine apps. Add setup offers USB-C, camera Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi and Hotspot. DISP 3 returns with a REC chip, large timecode and setting tiles; tap to change, hold and drag to reorder.
- Media is faster on large cards: listing, selecting, Select all, transfer progress and playback no longer stall. Full Resolution Caching keeps a played clip downloading after you close it and marks it CACHED.
- Tap storage to cycle free space, free percentage and estimated time in either orientation. In Photo, SHOTS shows a short count such as 6.7k; tap for free space and percentage.
- Instant review reads RAW previews, appears sooner for RAW, JPEG and RAW+JPEG in every exposure mode, and RAW+JPEG reviews turn sharp.
- FPS reads LINK until the first picture, then the frame rate. Landscape places the colour profile beside the codec; portrait uses three readout columns. Diagnostics distinguish incoming video, decoding and display interruptions.

Fixes

- The live picture stays smooth after connecting; running timecode no longer bogs it down. Live View keeps camera readouts and timecode frames through warm restarts and Quality Bias changes.
- Background recovery keeps your stills or video mode, and no longer keeps the camera busy while the app is in the background.
- A dozing camera gets a wait of up to about 20 s; half-press the shutter to connect. A silent camera says it may be asleep instead of showing a connection error.
- Take Over asks first; the phone losing the camera stops reconnecting. Refused Wi-Fi joins offer Settings, explicit retries ask again, and abandoned connections stop behind the setup screen.
- Exposure readouts return: Auto ISO shows its limit, program-owned values are dimmed and locked, hold SHUTTER to toggle its lock, and Reading camera settings shows at startup.
- Photo disables LUTs and cinema tools; video restores them. Shots left and app-triggered photos use the stills card. In CL/CH, tap for one photo or hold for a burst.
- Sharing treats photos as photos and finishes copying clips first. Playback stays paused after Share or app return. GYRO clears the screen lock. Safe timed pulls warn on overruns; Stop and safety checks remain. Closing Focus restores settings when idle and preserves manual changes.
- VoiceOver skips collapsed or covered controls; shutter labels describe actions. Setup asks for Local Network at its own step. Landscape View Assist hides covered values; focus setup reopens from Clean display and tap-to-focus no longer leaves pulls busy.

What to test

- Save two Native AF subjects and try both directions, including low contrast. For Timed Rack, set sharp A/B marks, Prepare lens with recording stopped, then compare 1, 3 and 6 second targets both ways. Check expected time, sharpness and steps; try Pause, Resume, Stop, Clean display and manual settings after closing. Two lenses have been tried; more testing is needed.
- Rotate in video mode and inspect DISP 3, FPS, timecode and storage, then Photo SHOTS. Change Quality Bias and background/return. If live view drops, Share Diagnostics with the time and action.
- Try RAW, JPEG and RAW+JPEG instant review in A, S, M and P. In CL/CH, tap then hold. Check the stills card and Photo/video LUT behavior.
- On a full card, scroll Media, select, Select all and cache clips. With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip briefly, go back and wait about 30 s: it reads CACHED.
- Connect a dozing camera; check wake-up or asleep guidance. Retry a cancelled Wi-Fi join. With two phones, Take Over asks first and the first phone stops.
