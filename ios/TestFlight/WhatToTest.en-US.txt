New and changed

- Focus now offers Native AF and Timed Rack. Save two subjects and repeat a focus change from your phone when filming alone.
- Native AF selects saved targets in the live picture. The camera controls the transition; set its g6 AF speed on the camera.
- Timed Rack offers A/B marks, duration and start delay. Prepare lens measures timing in one pass between the marks while recording is stopped.
- The compact panel shows saved marks, requested and expected timing, and playback. Edit marks holds Near/Far controls; Options holds the extra settings.

Fixes

- A dozing camera gets a waiting step of up to about 20 s; half-press the shutter during it to connect. A camera that stays silent says it may be asleep.
- A safe timed pull can finish after its requested duration and show a warning. Stop, cancellation and camera safety checks still apply.
- Saved Native AF targets and focus-point unlock are easier to see. Focus setup reopens from Clean display, and nudge progress stays in the pressed button.
- Automatic focus setup restores settings after closing when recording and focus have stopped, while keeping manual changes.

What to test

- In Native AF, save two subjects and try both directions. Check low-contrast subjects too; autofocus may need another target or a retry.
- In Timed Rack, set sharp A/B marks. With recording stopped, use Prepare lens once and confirm the other mark is sharp. Compare 1, 3 and 6 second targets in both directions; check the recording and the displayed expected time.
- Try Pause, Resume and Stop, then reopen setup from Clean display. Close the panel and check that manual focus-setting changes are kept.
- Repeat with your own lenses and report sharpness, visible steps, endpoint drift and any timing warning. Two lenses have been tried; more testing is needed.
