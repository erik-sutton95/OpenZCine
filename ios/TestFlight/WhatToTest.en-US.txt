New and changed

- Instant review of a RAW photo shows the preview the camera stores inside the file, without downloading the whole RAW.
- In portrait, the large format readout now lines up exactly with the clock at the top of the screen.
- Connecting to a saved Wi-Fi camera that the camera list cannot see now wakes it first, and reports that it is not on the network if it stays silent.

Fixes

- In Photo mode the LUT turns off again, so the stills preview shows the camera's colours. Switching back to video brings your LUT back.
- Instant review now works for RAW-only photos and appears sooner for JPEG and RAW+JPEG, in every exposure mode.
- The GYRO status pill no longer sits on the screen lock, so tapping it cannot grey out the controls. Gyro logging only arms once the camera reports video mode.
- Tapping to focus while the focus dial is driving no longer leaves focus stuck with a camera busy message.
- After the landscape assist palette collapses, its hidden buttons are no longer reachable with VoiceOver. A volume-button remote shutter is protected against a rare quit.
- With two cards, the shots counter counts only the card the camera saves photos to, and photos taken from the app go to that card.
- On a fresh install, the Local Network permission is asked for only at the permissions step of setup, not on the first screen.

What to test

- In video, turn a LUT on, switch the camera to Photo and check the preview colours match the camera, then switch back to video and check the LUT returns.
- Turn on instant review (PLAY). Shoot RAW, JPEG and RAW+JPEG in A, S, M and P; check each review turns sharp. With photos on card 2, compare shots left and where app photos land.
- With gyro logging on, tap the GYRO pill in landscape and portrait. It should open the gyro logs and never lock the controls.
- Reinstall: the Local Network prompt should appear only at the permissions step. Then connect to a saved Wi-Fi camera while it is waking from power saving.
- Turn the on-screen focus dial, then tap a new focus point mid-turn, several times. Focus should keep responding without a busy message. In portrait, check the format readout and clock align.
